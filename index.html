Tonavue Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Tonavue is an Android app for trying makeup on your own face, using the camera or a photo you choose. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Contact: selfiecoach@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Your camera frames, photos and face data are processed on your device and are never uploaded by Tonavue.
  • Tonavue has no accounts and no cloud storage, and we run no servers that receive your data.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to deliver them, as described below.

Camera, photos and face data

To place makeup, Tonavue detects facial landmarks (points such as lip and eye outlines), estimates skin tone and lighting, and separates hair and hands from the face. All of this happens on your device, in memory, while you use the app.

  • Camera. Used only while Live Mirror is open, with your permission. Frames are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos. You pick a photo with the Android photo picker. Tonavue can read only the photo you pick; it does not request access to your gallery.
  • Face data. Landmarks and skin estimates are discarded when you leave the screen. They are not used to identify you, are not turned into a face template, and are not shared with anyone, including ad providers.

What is stored on your device

  • Saved looks and your shade palette (colours and settings only, not your photo).
  • App settings and your last editing session.
  • If you turn on photo history in Settings (off by default), copies of photos you edited, kept in the app's private storage.
  • Images you choose to export, saved to your gallery under Pictures/Tonavue. Location and other photo metadata are not written to them.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and never leaves your device unless you share an image yourself.

Advertising

Tonavue is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear in two places: one sponsored card in the look gallery, and an optional rewarded ad that you can choose to watch to unlock high-resolution export and the 4-up compare board for 24 hours. No ad is shown over the camera or the editor.

The AdMob SDK is the only part of the app that uses the internet. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

Your choices

  • Ad consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings under Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete your data. Settings → Clear all data removes saved looks, palette, photo history and settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Exported images in your gallery are yours to delete.
  • Permissions. You can revoke camera permission in Android settings; photo editing keeps working without it.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive or hold it. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, through AdMob, as described above.

Children

Tonavue is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Data the app stores stays in its private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. Ad requests made by the AdMob SDK are encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
